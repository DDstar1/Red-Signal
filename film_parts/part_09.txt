FILM PART 9 - THE NIGHTMARE: SLEEPWALKING

Night-vision style shot. Lyra Vex sleepwalks barefoot in simple dark sleepwear through a dim corridor, eyes wide and unfocused, movements slow and automatic. She stops at the surveillance wall, looking at her own face on the screens like a stranger. Deep blacks, single harsh light, red pulse at frame edges. Film grain.

Audio: low distorted whisper, barefoot steps, static heartbeat.

FILM NOTE: The first glitch. The programming isn't perfect - and the cracks show at night when she can't control them.
