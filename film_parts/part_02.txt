FILM PART 2 - THE RUINS: EXTRACTION RUN

Handheld shot in rain-soaked ruins. Lyra Vex, worn grey tactical jacket soaked, mud and ash streaked on her clothing, clutches her ribs (bruise on her side) as she moves through shattered concrete. Cold scattered rain falls like ash. Pulsing red emergency strobes and distant fires are the only color. Tense survival energy, fast cutting, film grain.

Audio: ragged breath, rain on concrete, distant groaning metal.

FILM NOTE: Establish the world rules - no sun, no warmth, only red. Show she's hurt and hiding it.
