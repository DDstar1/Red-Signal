FILM PART 12 - THE FAILSAFE: CHIP GLOW

Extreme close-up. A small data chip glows green in Lyra Vex's trembling hand. Blood trickles from her nose. Her vision tunnels as her knees buckle in a dim archive corridor. Deep blacks, the green glow illuminating her sharp face, red pulse at frame edges. Film grain.

Audio: rising electronic hum, distorted whispers, a thud as she hits the floor.

FILM NOTE: The failsafe activation - the moment the programming takes over her body. Blood from the nose is her tell. This is the turning point of Act 1.
