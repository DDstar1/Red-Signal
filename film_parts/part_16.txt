FILM PART 16 - THE RESOLVE: ACT 1 FINALE

Medium shot. Lyra Vex, still trembling, pushes herself up off the archive floor. Her expression hardens - no longer blank, no longer theirs. She grips the data chip. Cold light on one side, warm red on the other, splitting her face. Slow push-in. Film grain.

Audio: her breath steadying, a rising low tone, a distant red klaxon.

LINE: 'She wasn't afraid of the Council anymore. She wasn't theirs.'

FILM NOTE: The two-tone signature shot - teal on one side, red on the other. Lyra's internal conflict made visible. She is no longer a weapon. She is a survivor.
