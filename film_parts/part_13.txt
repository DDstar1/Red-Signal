FILM PART 13 - THE ARCHIVE: ARCHIVE C

Slow push-in through a dust-choked secret archive. Towering shelves of physical files and film canisters. Kael walks Lyra Vex through the files; Tomas watches from the doorway. Beams of light cut through floating dust. Cold ambient light, deep blacks, faint red glow at the far edge. Film grain.

Audio: settling dust, footsteps on old metal, a distant heartbeat.

FILM NOTE: The truth room. No network, no trace - the Council's deepest secret stored in paper. This is where Lyra reads who she really is.
