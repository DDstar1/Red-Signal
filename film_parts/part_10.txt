FILM PART 10 - THE QUESTION: INTERROGATION

Over-the-shoulder shot in a stark interrogation room. Kael leans forward across the table, cold eyes fixed on Lyra Vex. She sits composed, wide pupils, giving nothing. Single harsh interrogation lamp, long shadows, red pulse at the frame edge. Film grain.

Audio: low hum, chair creak, heavy silence.

FILM NOTE: Two interrogators. Kael asks questions - but Lyra's stillness interrogates him right back. The power shifts subtly here.
