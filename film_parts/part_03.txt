FILM PART 3 - FIRST CONTACT: JOSIE'S PATROL

Two-shot in a ruined street, rain falling like ash. Lyra Vex, defensive stance, blood-streaked. Josie Kael, young Order soldier in dark matte armor, lowers his rifle slowly, extending an open hand. Warm cautious eyes on his soft youthful face. Teal-grey grade, faint red strobe, shallow depth of field, film grain.

Audio: rain, tense silence, a quiet exhale.

LINE: 'We're not going to kill you.'

FILM NOTE: The emotional hinge. Josie's first act of trust is what saves her. Frame it so his open hand is the brightest thing in the shot.
