FILM PART 19 - ACT 2: WREN'S HUNT

Tracking shot. Wren Avo, visor down, faceless menace, leads a squad of black-armored soldiers through the base, searching for Lyra Vex. Red emergency light pulses across her dark visor. Deliberate, cold, methodical. Film grain.

Audio: heavy boot steps, comm static, the pulse of the alarm.

FILM NOTE: With the visor down, Wren is pure threat. The audience knows she's hunting the protagonist - every step raises tension.
