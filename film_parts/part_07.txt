FILM PART 7 - THE TRAINING DECK: SHADOW RUN

Wide handheld shot. Lyra Vex, borrowed combat vest over a dark base layer, moves through an obstacle course like a shadow - smooth, fast, automatic, no wasted motion. Soldiers and officers watch in silence. Harsh overhead fluorescents, long shadows, faint red emergency glow. Film grain.

Audio: sharp footfalls, quick breaths, the hum of the deck.

FILM NOTE: The reveal. Nobody trains a refugee to fight like this. Every onlooker's silence says what no one will voice.
