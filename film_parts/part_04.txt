FILM PART 4 - ARRIVAL: THE ORDER BASE

Medium tracking shot as Lyra Vex, wet clothing steaming in decontamination, walks into the Order base. Exhausted but standing straight. Josie Kael escorts her; Wren Avo watches from a platform above, visor raised, assessing. Brutalist concrete and steel, dim amber task lights, cold blue-grey fluorescents. Film grain.

Audio: echoing footsteps, machinery hum, quiet comm chatter.

FILM NOTE: Introduce the base as a character - industrial, functional, watchful. Wren's first suspicious look happens here.
