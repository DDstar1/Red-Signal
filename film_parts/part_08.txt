FILM PART 8 - THE ANOMALY: TOMAS' FIRST LOOK

Medium shot. Tomas Vale, a tired disheveled analyst, hunches over a bank of monitors in the control hub, rapid typing. Data streams reflect in his observant tired eyes. A biometric file for Lyra Vex shows on screen - readings too clean, too calibrated. He stops typing. Cold monitor glow, dim room, faint red alert flicker. Film grain.

Audio: rapid keystrokes fading to silence, low machine hum.

LINE: 'Pixels don't twitch when they lie.'

FILM NOTE: The mystery hook. Tomas sees what no one else does - the file is too perfect. He will chase this for the rest of Act 1.
