FILM PART 5 - THE ASSESSMENT: KAEL'S DESK

Over-the-shoulder shot. Commander Ivar Kael, a large imposing man in his 50s, sits behind a heavy desk, cold assessing eyes fixed on Lyra Vex across from him. She sits perfectly still, unnervingly calm, wide pupils. Warm amber task lamp, long shadows, faint red alert glow. Slow push-in on Kael. Film grain.

Audio: low rumble, a chair creak, heavy silence.

FILM NOTE: Power dynamics - Kael looms, Lyra gives nothing. Neither blinks. This is where Kael's suspicion starts.
