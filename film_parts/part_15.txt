FILM PART 15 - THE BREAK: COLLAPSE

Close-up. Lyra Vex against a wall in a dim archive room, shoulders folding inward, trembling hands covering her face, blood at her nose. Wide haunted eyes above her fingers. Single harsh light from above, deep shadows, red pulse at frame edges. Film grain.

Audio: ragged breathing, a muffled sob, the hum of the archive.

LINE: 'You're more than it.'

FILM NOTE: The emotional hinge of Act 1. Years of conditioning suppress this - so when it breaks, it's devastating. Tomas' line is the first kindness from someone who knows the truth.
