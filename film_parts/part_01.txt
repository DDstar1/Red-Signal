FILM PART 1 - PROLOGUE: THE HIDDEN BUNKER

Wide shot, smoke and haze, dim flickering light. Lyra Vex, a young woman in her mid-20s with short dark hair and a worn dark grey tactical jacket, adjusts her collar, concealing a small data drive beneath it. She stands in a hidden bunker, resolute expression, controlled upright posture. Cold ash-rain seeps through cracks above. Deep blacks, desaturated grade, faint red glow from a dying monitor. Film grain.

Audio: muffled rain, a distant siren, the soft creak of the jacket.

FILM NOTE: This is the establishing beat - Lyra the operative, the drive, the lie she believes. Keep her face neutral-calm, not afraid.
