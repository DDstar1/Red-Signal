FILM PART 6 - FIRST NIGHT: THE DORM

Wide shot of Lyra Vex alone in a small Order dorm, standing by a narrow window, cold blue-grey light on her face. Her worn jacket hangs on a chair. She touches her collar where the drive is hidden. Behind her, in the doorway, Josie Kael stands with arms folded, back against the wall - guarding, waiting. Warm task lamp, cold window light. Film grain.

Audio: soft ambient hum, distant footsteps, her quiet breath.

FILM NOTE: Two people in one frame but not together. Josie's protective distance, Lyra's guarded isolation.
