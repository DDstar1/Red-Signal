FILM PART 14 - THE TRUTH: READING THE FILE

Close-up over Lyra Vex's shoulder as she reads a physical file in Archive C. Her wide pupils track the words: PROJECT ECHO. SUBJECT DESIGNATION. FALSE MEMORIES. Her hands tremble slightly. Tomas watches her reaction from the doorway, arms folded. Cold dust-lit air, film grain.

Audio: paper rustling, her quickened breath, the low hum of the archive.

LINE (whisper): 'I don't know where I'm from anymore.'

FILM NOTE: The reveal lands on her face, not the page. Let the silence do the work.
