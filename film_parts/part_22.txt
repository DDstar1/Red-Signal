FILM PART 22 - ACT 2: THE SIGNAL

Wide shot. Lyra Vex stands in the rain-soaked ruins at the edge of the base, back to camera, worn grey jacket, short dark hair. She turns her head slightly as a red signal pulses on the horizon. The camera holds on her silhouette. Rain falls like ash. Film grain.

Audio: wind, ash-rain, a soft heartbeat.

LINE: 'I don't know where I'm from anymore.'

FILM NOTE: The closing image of the month-long arc - the red signal that started the story, now behind her. She's out, but not free. The signal stays on.
