FILM PART 18 - ACT 2: JOSIE'S CHOICE

Close two-shot in a red-lit corridor. Josie Kael, hair sticking up in wild tufts, out of breath, disheveled, rushes to Lyra Vex's side. He looks between her and the direction of the alarm - duty vs. conscience. He makes his choice and steps in front of her. Red emergency light strobing, film grain.

Audio: heavy breathing, klaxons, the creak of armor.

FILM NOTE: Josie's Act 2 disheveled look - wild hair, heaving chest. The choice is written on his face before he speaks.
