FILM PART 17 - ACT 2: THE ALARM

Handheld shot in an Order corridor as red alert lockdown begins. Pulsing red emergency lights strobe across concrete walls, soldiers in matte black armor move in formation, metallic doors slam shut. Lyra Vex, worn grey jacket, moves against the flow. Frantic energy, film grain.

Audio: blaring klaxons, boot steps, slamming metal, distant shouting.

FILM NOTE: Act 2 opens in motion - the truth is out, the base is hunting, and Lyra is on the run. The red signal now works against her.
