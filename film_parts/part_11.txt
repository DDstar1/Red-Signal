FILM PART 11 - THE KINDNESS: THE JACKET

Close two-shot in a cold Order dorm room. Josie Kael wraps his coat around Lyra Vex's shoulders. Her pale face softens, intense eyes meeting his warm gaze. Cold blue-grey window light, warm amber lamp, shallow depth of field, slow push-in. Film grain.

Audio: soft room tone, fabric rustling, a shared quiet breath.

FILM NOTE: The crack in the programming. Lyra has never been touched with kindness - she doesn't know what to do with it. This is where she starts to become a person, not a weapon.
